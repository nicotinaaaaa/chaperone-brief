This brief covers biology, biotech and pharma news from about Oct 25 to Nov 1, 2026. The Oct 25 brief already covered Merck/Moderna's melanoma vaccine, Roivant's brepocitinib, Disc Medicine, Rapport, GSK/Ionis' Hibsago, Merck KGaA–Silence and Gilead–Vir, so those are not repeated. **Coverage note:** many trade-press sites block automated access. I include only details I could confirm and say where a number, stock move or outcome was not available. Prices are as reported by the cited source and may be intraday. This is not investment advice.

**Terms used throughout.**
- **Clinical trial phases.** *Phase 1* is a small first-in-human study focused on safety. *Phase 2* looks for early signs the drug works. *Phase 3* is a large study, usually against placebo or standard care, and is the main basis for approval.
- **Topline results** are the first headline numbers a company releases, before full data are presented or peer-reviewed.
- **Interim analysis** is a planned early look at the data before the trial ends. If the result is strong enough, the trial can be declared successful early.
- **Endpoint** is the measurement a trial uses to judge success. *Overall survival (OS)* is how long patients live. *Progression-free survival (PFS)* is how long patients live without their cancer growing. *Objective response rate (ORR)* is the share of patients whose tumors shrink by a set amount.
- **Hazard ratio (HR)** compares the rate of an event (death, say) between two groups over time. An HR of 0.50 means half the risk of the comparison group. A *95% confidence interval (CI)* is the range the true value likely falls in; narrower means more precise.
- **Odds ratio** compares the odds of something happening in two groups; 3.17 means roughly three times the odds.
- **Open-label** means patients and doctors know which treatment is being given (no blinding).
- **Placebo** is a dummy treatment.
- **Accelerated approval** is an FDA route that approves a drug early based on a measurement thought likely to predict real benefit (a "surrogate"), on condition that the company later confirms benefit in a further trial. The FDA can withdraw approval if it fails.
- **PDUFA date** is the FDA's target deadline for deciding on a drug application.
- **Bispecific antibody** is an engineered antibody with two different "grabbing" arms that can bind two different targets at once.
- **PD-L1 / checkpoint inhibitor.** Some tumors display a protein called PD-L1 that switches off attacking immune cells. A *checkpoint inhibitor* such as Keytruda (pembrolizumab) blocks that signal. A *CPS* (combined positive score) of 1 or more means at least some PD-L1 is detected.
- **Antibody-drug conjugate (ADC).** An antibody that homes in on cancer cells, carrying a chemical poison that is released inside them.
- **Upfront payment, milestones, royalties.** In a licensing deal these are, respectively, money paid at signing, money paid only if goals (trial results, approvals) are met, and a percentage of future sales.

## 1. Clinical trials

### Genmab's petosemtamab plus Keytruda halves the risk of death in head and neck cancer; shares jump ~30% to a record
**What happened.** On Oct 28, Genmab announced positive topline results from the Phase 3 LiGeR-HN1 trial. It tested petosemtamab plus Keytruda against Keytruda alone as first treatment in people with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma whose tumors show PD-L1 (CPS ≥1). At the first interim analysis the combination cut the risk of death by about 50% (HR 0.50; 95% CI 0.37–0.67; p<0.0001) and raised the odds of tumor response about 3.2-fold (odds ratio 3.17; 95% CI 2.06–4.89). Benefit was seen regardless of HPV status. The company reported no new safety signals; absolute survival months, response rates and enrollment were not given, and full data will come at a future medical meeting. Genmab shares rose nearly 30% in the session, to an all-time high and the biggest one-day gain in its history, with the stock up more than 80% this year. A weekly roundup shows GMAB closing Thursday Oct 29 at $56.49, down 1.4% after the surge. The Fierce Biotech headline calls this a payoff for Genmab's roughly $8 billion purchase of Merus (the company that developed the drug); I could not open the article to confirm the price.

**Biology.** Head and neck squamous cell carcinoma is cancer of the flat lining cells of the mouth, throat, voice box and nearby areas. It is linked to tobacco, heavy alcohol use and the virus HPV, and causes pain, trouble swallowing or speaking, and a lump in the neck. Once it has come back or spread, it is rarely curable. Petosemtamab is a bispecific antibody that grabs two proteins on cancer cells at once: EGFR (a receptor many tumors rely on for growth signals) and LGR5 (a marker on cancer stem-like cells that help tumors renew). Keytruda separately releases the immune system's brake.

**Why it matters.** Keytruda alone is the current first-line standard for this group, and a 50% cut in death risk on top of it is a very large effect for this disease. Genmab is also running more Phase 3 trials in later-line head and neck cancer and in colorectal cancer. The prior week Genmab also reported that Rina-S, an ADC for ovarian cancer, met its Phase 3 PFS goal with a 71% reduction in the risk of progression or death (details not independently confirmed).
**Sources:** [OncLive (Oct 28, 2026)](https://www.onclive.com/view/petosemtamab-plus-pembrolizumab-hits-phase-3-os-orr-primary-end-points-in-pd-l1-positive-r-m-hnscc); [Stocktwits (Oct 28)](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/gmab-stock-hits-all-time-high-after-genmab-cancer-drug-trial-meets-primary-goals/cZU9Nu2R9Gh); [RTTNews (Oct 28)](https://www.rttnews.com/3704535/genmab-shares-surge-30-on-phase-3-head-and-neck-cancer-trial-data.aspx); [RTTNews Weekly Buzz (Oct 30)](https://www.rttnews.com/amp/3706690/weekly-buzz-abbv-pfe-win-fda-nod-artv-slashes-jobs-davi-sgry-strike-deals.aspx); [Fierce Biotech headline](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/genmabs-head-and-neck-cancer-bispecific-cuts-risk-death-half-ph3). **Stage:** Phase 3 topline, positive; market-relevant.

### Beam's base-editing therapy BEAM-301 shows early signs of working in a rare metabolic disease
**What happened.** Beam presented first Phase 1/2 data for BEAM-301 at the European Society of Gene & Cell Therapy congress (poster Oct 28; investor webcast Oct 27). Six patients received a single infusion (three at 20 mg, three at 40 mg; data cut June 26, 2026). Time they could go without eating before blood sugar dropped dangerously low rose in a dose-dependent way: in the 20 mg group from a median of 2.9 hours to 4.3 hours by day 36, and both evaluable 40 mg patients reached the protocol's maximum of at least 15 hours. Their prescribed daily starch intake fell by 71–92% in most reported time points, and 11% at day 387 in the 20 mg group. Safety: five serious adverse events, all resolved within 2–10 days, including three grade 3 infusion reactions linked to the steroid pre-treatment (since adjusted); no deaths, treatment discontinuations or dose-limiting toxicities. Share reaction on the data days was not available; on the Oct 13 date it announced the presentation, the stock closed down 2.8%.

**Biology.** Glycogen storage disease type Ia (GSDIa) is an inherited disorder in which the liver lacks a working enzyme (glucose-6-phosphatase) needed to release stored sugar into the blood. Babies and children get dangerous low blood sugar within hours of not eating, so patients must eat cornstarch every few hours, including through the night. Over time it can cause liver tumors, kidney damage and growth problems. *Base editing* rewrites a single letter of DNA without cutting both strands. Beam's version is delivered inside *lipid nanoparticles* (tiny fat bubbles that carry the editing machinery to the liver) to fix the faulty gene.

**Why it matters.** It is among the first looks at a one-time gene fix for this disease, and proof that base editors can work in the liver. The sample is tiny and follow-up is short at the higher dose, so durability and infusion-reaction management remain open questions.
**Sources:** [BiopharmaWatch summary of Beam release](https://www.biopharmawatch.com/news/BEAM/beam-therapeutics-beam-301-gsdi-trial-data-esgct); [Stocktwits Pharma Pulse (Yahoo Finance)](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/stocktwits-pharma-pulse-big-pharma-022829910.html). **Stage:** Phase 1/2, early clinical.

### Oncology data and setbacks (grouped)
- **Summit's ivonescimab in biliary tract cancer (reported Oct 25).** In a Phase 3 trial run in China, ivonescimab plus chemotherapy cut the risk of death by 25% versus durvalumab plus chemotherapy; median survival was 14.9 vs 12.1 months at an interim analysis. Biliary tract cancer starts in the bile ducts or gallbladder, is usually found late, and has a poor outlook. Ivonescimab is a bispecific antibody that blocks both PD-1 (the immune brake targeted by checkpoint inhibitors) and VEGF (a signal tumors use to grow new blood vessels). *Source:* [Stocktwits Pharma Pulse](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/stocktwits-pharma-pulse-big-pharma-022829910.html). **Stage:** Phase 3 interim (China only; U.S. relevance uncertain).
- **MacroGenics' lorigerlimab in clear cell gynecologic cancer.** Phase 2 interim: 25% of patients had confirmed tumor shrinkage, 44% had disease control, and median PFS was 2.9 months. Clear cell gynecologic cancers are uncommon ovarian or uterine tumors that respond poorly to chemotherapy. Lorigerlimab is a bispecific antibody blocking PD-1 and CTLA-4 (a second immune brake). Shares closed at $2.38, down 3.6%. *Source:* [RTTNews Weekly Buzz (Oct 30)](https://www.rttnews.com/amp/3706690/weekly-buzz-abbv-pfe-win-fda-nod-artv-slashes-jobs-davi-sgry-strike-deals.aspx). **Stage:** Phase 2, modest.
- **Geron halts its Phase 3 myelofibrosis trial.** An independent safety board concluded imetelstat was unlikely to meet its overall-survival goal in IMpactMF, so Geron stopped the study. Myelofibrosis is a bone-marrow cancer in which scar tissue crowds out blood-cell production, causing severe anemia, an enlarged spleen and fatigue. Imetelstat blocks telomerase, an enzyme that cancer cells use to keep dividing indefinitely. Geron keeps its approved drug Rytelo (for lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes, in which bone marrow makes faulty blood cells). Shares closed at $0.94, down 2.3%, after what appears to be a long decline. *Source:* [RTTNews Weekly Buzz](https://www.rttnews.com/amp/3706690/weekly-buzz-abbv-pfe-win-fda-nod-artv-slashes-jobs-davi-sgry-strike-deals.aspx). **Stage:** Phase 3 discontinued; market-relevant.

### Other trial readouts (grouped)
- **AbbVie's Qulipta (atogepant) beats topiramate in migraine prevention.** In the Phase 3 TEMPLE head-to-head, 12% of Qulipta patients stopped because of side effects versus 30% on topiramate, and more had substantial cuts in monthly migraine days. Migraine is a recurring neurological disorder causing severe, often one-sided headache, nausea and sensitivity to light and sound. Atogepant blocks the CGRP receptor (CGRP is a pain-signaling protein that surges during attacks); topiramate is an older anti-seizure drug commonly used for prevention but poorly tolerated. *Source:* RTTNews Weekly Buzz. **Stage:** Phase 3, positive.
- **Benitec's BB-301 in oculopharyngeal muscular dystrophy (OPMD).** A Phase 1b/2a study reported a 100% response rate on swallowing function at both doses, with no serious adverse events. OPMD is a rare inherited muscle disease that starts in midlife with drooping eyelids and progressive difficulty swallowing, leading to choking and weight loss. BB-301 is a gene therapy designed to silence the faulty gene and supply a working copy. Very small study; "response" was not defined in the source. Shares closed at $8.83, up 0.3%. *Source:* RTTNews Weekly Buzz. **Stage:** Phase 1b/2a, early.
- **60 Degrees Pharmaceuticals in babesiosis.** In an expanded-access (compassionate-use) study, all five patients who completed treatment improved and tested negative by standard PCR. Babesiosis is a tick-borne infection by a malaria-like parasite that invades red blood cells, causing fever, chills and anemia and posing serious risk to older or immunocompromised people. The placebo-controlled trial unblinds in January 2027. Five patients, no control group. *Source:* RTTNews Weekly Buzz. **Stage:** Preclinical-to-early clinical; micro-cap.

## 2. Regulatory decisions

### Inovio wins accelerated FDA approval for VOCERYQ, a DNA medicine for a rare airway disease
**What happened.** On Oct 30, Inovio announced the FDA granted accelerated approval to VOCERYQ (formerly INO-3107) to reduce how often adults with recurrent respiratory papillomatosis (RRP) need surgery. In the supporting study of 32 adults, 7 of the 26 patients with at least three surgeries in the prior year (27%; 95% CI 12–48%) had a complete response, meaning no surgeries over 12 months of follow-up. Inovio must submit a confirmatory-trial protocol by the end of 2026. Common side effects were injection-site reactions, headache, fatigue, high blood pressure, nausea and dizziness; patients must be watched 30 minutes after injection, and the treatment should be avoided in people with pacemakers or implanted defibrillators on the same side. Product should ship in about 60 days. The Weekly Buzz said Inovio shares "soared"; I could not find the percentage. A third-party site cites about $37 million in liquidity and a roughly four-month cash runway (not confirmed from Inovio's own filings), so a capital raise is likely.

**Biology.** RRP is a rare condition in which HPV types 6 and 11 cause wart-like growths (papillomas) on the voice box and airway. They return after removal, hoarsening the voice and, if untreated, blocking breathing, so many patients undergo dozens of surgeries over a lifetime. VOCERYQ is a *DNA medicine*: circular DNA strands encoding HPV proteins are injected into muscle and pushed into cells by a small electrical pulse (*electroporation*, via the CELLECTRA device), teaching the immune system to attack HPV-infected cells.

**Why it matters.** It is the first approval of Inovio's DNA-vaccine platform after years of setbacks,. A 27% complete-response rate on a small single-arm study (no comparison group) is why approval is conditional.
**Sources:** [BiopharmaWatch (Oct 30, 2026)](https://www.biopharmawatch.com/news/INO/inovio-fda-accelerated-approval-voceryq-rrp); [RTTNews Weekly Buzz](https://www.rttnews.com/amp/3706690/weekly-buzz-abbv-pfe-win-fda-nod-artv-slashes-jobs-davi-sgry-strike-deals.aspx). **Stage:** approved (conditional); market-relevant.

### Other approvals (grouped; all reported in the Oct 30 Weekly Buzz)
- **Pfizer's Talzenna plus Xtandi (FDA)** is now approved for metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer regardless of BRCA status. In Phase 3 TALAPRO-3 it cut the risk of radiographic progression or death by 52% versus Xtandi plus placebo. Prostate cancer grows in response to male hormones; "hormone-sensitive" means it still responds to hormone-blocking therapy. Xtandi blocks the androgen (male-hormone) receptor, and Talzenna is a PARP inhibitor, which stops cancer cells repairing their own DNA damage. BRCA is a gene whose faults cripple DNA repair and make cancers more PARP-sensitive. PFE closed up 2.9%.
- **AbbVie's Skyrizi (FDA)** can now be started at home with an on-body injector in moderate-to-severe Crohn's disease, and the FDA dropped a required tuberculosis test before treatment. Crohn's is a chronic autoimmune inflammation of the digestive tract causing pain, diarrhea, weight loss and bowel damage. Skyrizi blocks IL-23, an inflammation-signaling protein. ABBV closed up 1.7%.
- **AstraZeneca/Daiichi's Enhertu (EU)** was approved after surgery for HER2-positive early breast cancer with residual disease after pre-surgery treatment: 53% lower risk of recurrence or death than Kadcyla (3-year invasive disease-free survival 92.4% vs 83.7%). HER2-positive tumors carry too much HER2, a growth-signal protein. Both drugs are ADCs.
- **J&J's Tecvayli (EU)** as a single drug for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after at least one prior therapy: 71% lower risk of progression or death and 40% lower risk of death than standard care. Multiple myeloma is cancer of antibody-making plasma cells in bone marrow, causing bone damage, anemia, kidney problems and infections. Tecvayli is a bispecific antibody linking T cells to BCMA, a protein on myeloma cells. JNJ closed up 1.5%.
- **Aardvark's ARD-101: FDA clinical hold lifted (Oct 22).** Prader-Willi syndrome is a rare genetic disorder, present from birth, whose hallmark is hyperphagia: constant, extreme hunger that can lead to severe obesity and life-threatening overeating. The company says the terminated Phase 3 HERO data show only a modest effect and one serious cardiac event tied to a drug interaction; it plans to ask the FDA about a new Phase 3 in 2027, possibly at a lower dose. Shares closed at $1.44, down 4.6%.

### Pending: Agios' mitapivat in sickle cell disease
The FDA's target date was Nov 1 under priority review and accelerated approval. I could not find an outcome as of this run. Sickle cell disease is an inherited blood disorder in which a faulty hemoglobin makes red blood cells stiff and crescent-shaped, so they block vessels (causing severe pain crises, organ damage and stroke) and die early (causing anemia). Mitapivat is a pill that activates an enzyme (pyruvate kinase) in red blood cells to boost their energy supply. Its confirmatory trial, REIGNITE, has begun dosing. *Source:* [Agios Q2 2026 release](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/agios-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-provides-business-update).

## 4. Basic science

### A 34-year-old mystery solved: how a fat-derived signal both summons immune cells and shields tissue
Researchers at Memorial Sloan Kettering reported in *Nature* (published Oct 7, 2026; the news write-up circulated in late October, so the paper itself is just outside the usual window) that a molecule called 5-oxoETE does two jobs. It attracts immune cells to sites of infection, and it also protects healthy tissue. Philipp Niethammer's team found that zebrafish lacking the receptor that detects 5-oxoETE (zebrafish were used because mice lack the receptor) developed gut inflammation without infection. Without the signal, gut cells could not clear damaged DNA building blocks using enzymes called NUDIX hydrolases, and normal gut bacteria then stressed them to death. They also identified DHRS7 as the gene that makes the enzyme producing 5-oxoETE, a source unknown since 1992.

**Biology.** 5-oxoETE is made from arachidonic acid, a fatty acid released from cell membranes under mechanical stress, and its production is boosted by oxidative stress (damage from reactive oxygen molecules). **Why it matters.** Inflammatory bowel disease (chronic gut inflammation) and asthma have been linked to disrupted signaling here, and the gene DHRS7 and its receptor gene OXER1 are mutated in some cancers. The authors suggest the pathway could be boosted to protect the gut from radiation or chemotherapy damage; that is speculation. **Stage:** basic research in zebrafish.
**Source:** [SciTechDaily](https://scitechdaily.com/scientists-solve-a-30-year-biological-mystery-and-the-discovery-could-change-how-we-treat-inflammation/) (article date not shown; Nature paper dated Oct 7, 2026).

## Top takeaways

1. **Bispecific antibodies are the dominant oncology story.** Genmab's EGFR/LGR5 antibody halved death risk, Summit's PD-1/VEGF antibody extended survival in China, and J&J's Tecvayli gained a broader EU label. Genmab's record jump rewards its large Merus purchase.
2. **Gene and DNA medicines are moving from promise to products.** Inovio won (conditional) approval and Beam showed first human data for a liver base editor, though both rest on very small studies.
3. **Accelerated approval keeps carrying rare-disease launches,** with confirmatory trials still ahead. Cash-poor developers such as Inovio may need to raise money soon.
4. **Failures and cuts continue for weaker assets:** Geron stopped a Phase 3, Artiva is laying off 94% of staff, and Tango trimmed 14%. Investors reward clear Phase 3 wins and punish thin or ambiguous data.
5. **Licensing deals favor preclinical assets with small upfronts.** Astellas–Audax is "up to $1.34 billion," but only $285 million is upfront or option money, and Phase 1 hasn't started.
6. **Sector sentiment is firm:** XBI rose 3.6% in the week to Oct 23, though several stock moves this week had no verified cause.
