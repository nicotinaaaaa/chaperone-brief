## Metabolic disease

### Oral dual agonist clears phase 2 with best-in-class weight loss

A 48-week, randomized, placebo-controlled trial of an oral small-molecule GLP-1/GIP dual agonist reported a mean 19% weight loss, edging out prior oral candidates. Discontinuation due to GI side effects was around 9%, in line with the injectable class.

Full write-up: [read the paper](https://example.com/oral-agonist-phase2).

> "This is the first oral candidate that looks genuinely competitive with injectable
> dual agonists on efficacy, not just convenience," one trial investigator said.

## Gene editing

### Base editing correction of PCSK9 LOF variant holds past 12 months in primates

Two groups, working independently, used adenine base editors delivered via lipid nanoparticle to install a loss-of-function edit in *PCSK9* in cynomolgus macaques. LDL cholesterol reductions of roughly 60% were sustained through the full 12-month follow-up with no detected off-target editing at predicted sites.

Open questions going forward:

1. Durability beyond 12 months
2. Editing efficiency in humans vs. NHPs, which has historically diverged
   - Delivery efficiency differences
   - Immune response to the editor itself
3. Regulatory path — somatic one-time edit vs. a chronic therapy

## Genomics infrastructure

### Human thymus cell atlas released

A large international consortium released a harmonized single-cell and spatial atlas of the human thymus, spanning fetal development through adulthood, aimed at accelerating work on T-cell selection and autoimmune disease.
